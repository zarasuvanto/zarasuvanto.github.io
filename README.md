# zarasuvanto.github.io

Video portfolio of Zara Suvanto, multimedia creative. Live at https://zarasuvanto.github.io

- `index.html` – the whole site. Video titles, credit lines and descriptions are in the `WORK` list near the bottom of the file.
- `videos/` – web-compressed MP4s (the background reel comes in 4K, 1080p and a vertical phone cut)
- `posters/` – cover frames for each video
- `sprites/` – 20-frame strips used for hover-to-scrub on the Cardboard Spaceship videos
- `img/` – logo, snake intro, headshot, reel stills
- `files/` – resume PDF
