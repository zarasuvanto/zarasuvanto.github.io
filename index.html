Firstake

Creator and project team lead, United AI at Syracuse

I built Firstake after spending hours and a lot of credits trying to get AI tools to make what I actually wanted, and it was my first time ever coding an app, which I did with Claude Code. Firstake is an AI creative director that tells people which AI tool to use for what they want to make, shows the best, cheapest and free options with checked prices, and writes a ready-to-paste prompt in that tool’s own language. I’m continuing to develop it with guidance from Syracuse’s Blackstone LaunchPad and the iSchool, and my goal is for Firstake to help students, creators and businesses spend less time and money on trial and error, so they can focus on what they’re making.

  1. Describe what you want to make.
  2. See the best, cheapest and free tools for it.
  3. Copy a prompt written for that tool.

About

Hi! I’m Zara Suvanto, a Television, Radio & Film student at Syracuse University’s Newhouse School, with a minor in Marketing. I’m super passionate about storytelling, being creative, and finding new ways to bring ideas to life through video, media, and technology!

This past summer, I started my own video production company, ZES Creative, creating video and social media content for local businesses in Westchester, NY. I actually found my first clients by going door to door! Running ZES has taught me so much about content creation, marketing my own business, and working directly with clients. It also got me thinking about how AI could make the creative process easier, which led me to develop Firstake, a creative tool focused on helping people bring their video ideas to life.

I also spent the summer as a video editing intern at Cardboard Spaceship, where I worked on commercial projects and gained hands-on experience in professional post-production. I also had the opportunity to explore new AI tools and learn how they’re changing the way creative content is produced.

My experiences have given me the chance to explore so many different sides of the industry, and I’m always excited to learn more! I’m currently looking for Summer 2027 internship opportunities in New York City across creative media, marketing, social media, production, entertainment, and the business side of the industry.
