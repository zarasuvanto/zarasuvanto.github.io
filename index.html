Firstake

Creator and project team lead, United AI at Syracuse

Firstake is an AI creative director. It tells people which AI tool to use for what they want to make, shows the best, cheapest and free options with checked prices, and writes a ready-to-paste prompt in that tool’s own language. I’m continuing to build it with guidance from Syracuse’s Blackstone LaunchPad and the iSchool, because AI is moving faster than most people can keep up with. Firstake saves students, creators and businesses hours of trial and error, and wasted money, by getting it right on the first take.

  1. Describe what you want to make.
  2. See the best, cheapest and free tools for it.
  3. Copy a prompt written for that tool.

About

I’m Zara, a Television, Radio & Film student at Syracuse University’s Newhouse School with a minor in marketing. I edit and direct video: commercial spots, short-form for brands, and news.

This year I started ZES Creative, a production company making video and social content for local businesses in Westchester. I found my first clients by going door to door, and I take every project from shot list to delivery. Over the summer I cut commercial spots as an editing intern at Cardboard Spaceship, and for two years I reported and produced weekly packages for CitrusTV News.

I’m most at home in the edit, finding the rhythm that makes a 30-second spot land. I’m looking for internships in post-production and video.
